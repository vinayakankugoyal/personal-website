# Bike Lane Dash

Riding a bike in New York means sharing a strip of green paint with police cars, double-parked trucks, cabs, people glued to their phones, and the occasional e-bike going the wrong way. This is a little endless runner about that. Every crash tells you what got you and how to avoid it in real life.

## What's in the lane

- **Car doors.** Painted bike lanes often run right through the door zone of parked cars. Look for people sitting in parked cars and give them a door's width.
- **Police cars and double-parked trucks.** When the lane is blocked you're allowed to leave it. Shoulder check, signal, and merge early.
- **Cabs.** Taxis and rideshares swerve into the bike lane for pickups. Watch for blinkers and people waving from the curb.
- **Pedestrians.** People step out from between parked cars, usually looking at a phone. Slow down and yield.
- **Wrong-way riders.** They close the gap fast. Keep right and use your bell.
- **Potholes.** Scan the pavement, not just the traffic, and report them to 311.

You can report blocked bike lanes and potholes to [311](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/).
