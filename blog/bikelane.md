# Bike Lane Dash

Riding a bike in New York means sharing a strip of green paint with police cars, double-parked trucks, cabs, people glued to their phones, and the occasional e-bike going the wrong way. This is a little endless runner about that. Every crash tells you what got you and how to avoid it in real life.

## What's in the lane

- **Car doors.** Painted bike lanes often run right through the door zone of parked cars. Look for people sitting in parked cars and give them a door's width.
- **Police cars and double-parked trucks.** When the lane is blocked you're allowed to leave it. Shoulder check, signal, and merge early.
- **Cabs.** Taxis and rideshares swerve into the bike lane for pickups. Watch for blinkers and people waving from the curb.
- **Pedestrians.** People step out from between parked cars, usually looking at a phone. Slow down and yield.
- **Wrong-way riders.** They close the gap fast. Keep right and use your bell.
- **Potholes.** Scan the pavement, not just the traffic, and report them to 311.

You can report blocked bike lanes and potholes to [311](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/).

## By the numbers

- 30 people were killed riding bikes in NYC in 2023, the most in over two decades. 23 of them were on e-bikes, and about 400 more riders were seriously injured. ([Fox 5](https://www.fox5ny.com/news/nyc-cyclist-deaths-hit-record-high-in-2023), [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/in-response-to-numerous-fatalities-new-york-city-launches-e-bike-safety-education-campaign), [News 12](https://bronx.news12.com/record-levels-of-cyclist-deaths-for-nyc-in-2023))
- Police wrote tickets for just 1.9% of the 76,000+ 311 complaints about blocked bike lanes filed between 2016 and 2023. ([Streetsblog](https://usa.streetsblog.org/2023/04/06/cops-in-nyc-ticket-only-2-of-blocked-bike-lanes-analysis))

## The rules

- **NY VTL §1214:** no one may open a car door on the traffic side until it's reasonably safe.
- **34 RCNY §4-08(e):** stopping, standing, or parking in a bike lane is banned, with a $115 fine.
- **34 RCNY §4-12(p):** ride in the bike lane when there is one, but you may leave it to turn or when it's blocked or unsafe. ([NYC DOT](https://nyc.gov/html/dot/downloads/pdf/bicyclerules-english.pdf))
- **NY VTL §1236:** at night you need a white front light visible from 500 ft and a red or amber rear light visible from 300 ft.
- **NY VTL §1238:** riders and passengers under 14 must wear a helmet.
- **NYC e-bike limit:** since October 24, 2025, e-bikes and e-scooters are capped at 15 mph citywide. ([NY1](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/news/2025/10/24/new-15-mile-per-hour-speed-limit-for-e-bikes-goes-into-effect))
